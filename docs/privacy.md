# Privacy policy

*Personal Assistant 101. Last updated October 30, 2026.*

Personal Assistant 101 is a personal project. I build it for myself and I'm its only user; it isn't offered to anyone else.

## What Google data it uses, and why

- **Google Calendar:** reads my events to answer questions like "what's on Friday?", and adds events I approve with a tap. It never changes or deletes an event.
- **Google Tasks:** adds tasks I approve with a tap.
- **Gmail:** reads my email, read-only, to answer questions about it and to learn how I write. It can't send, draft or delete email.
- **Google Drive (later):** reads my documents, read-only.

## How it's handled

- It's used only to answer my own requests and to suggest changes I approve. It isn't sold, used for ads, or shared with anyone.
- The app's data lives in its own private database. The parts of my Google data a request needs are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to answer it, and nowhere else. Anthropic's terms don't allow it to train models on that data.
- Server logs hold no content. The app's own log of what I asked and what it did is deleted after 7 days.
- I can delete what it saves, and remove its access to my Google account at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Assistant 101's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
